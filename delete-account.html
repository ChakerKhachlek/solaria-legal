Guardians of Solaria: delete your account and data

Developer: Chaker KHACHLEK, France · Last updated: 21 October 2026

How to ask

  • In the game: Settings › Delete account (when you are signed in with Google).
  • By email: write to solaria.guardians.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your display name with its tag (shown on your player card), or the email of the Google account you sign in with.

We confirm by email and delete within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player account and its link to your Google sign-in.
  • Your progress saved online (cloud save), your display name and its tag.
  • Your anonymous player id and the data linked to it at Unity Gaming Services.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play for as long as tax and consumer law require. Packs you bought stay tied to your Google account and come back with Restore purchases if you play again.
  • Your request email: kept for up to 2 years as proof that the deletion was done, then deleted.
  • Progress saved on your device: stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data (we cannot reach it).
