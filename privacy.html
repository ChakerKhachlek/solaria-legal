Guardians of Solaria: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Applies to the Android game "Guardians of Solaria" (package com.solaria.guardians)

In short

  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • Online features (co-op rooms, news notices, purchases) use Unity Gaming Services and Google Play. They work with an anonymous player id, not your name or contact details.
  • No ads, no advertising id, no sale of data, no chat.
  • Questions or deletion requests: solaria.guardians.support@gmail.com

Who is responsible

The game is developed and published by Chaker KHACHLEK, an individual developer based in France, who is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: solaria.guardians.support@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (Guardians, levels, items, gold, chests, pets, wings and other cosmetics, your display name and profile icon, read notices, settings) is saved in a file on your device. It stays there and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising id.
  • No sale or rental of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • No chat: rooms are joined only with a code you share yourself.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): running the online features you use and delivering the packs you buy.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)): keeping the online services secure, preventing purchase fraud and fixing crashes.
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)): keeping purchase records as tax and consumer law require (held by Google Play).

Transfers outside the European Union

Unity Technologies and Google may process data in the United States and other countries. They rely on the safeguards the GDPR allows, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • The anonymous player id and display name at Unity: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play for as long as the law requires.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to answer you, and at most 2 years.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Write to solaria.guardians.support@gmail.com; to find your online data, include your display name with its tag (shown on your player card). We answer within one month. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In France, players under 15 should use the online features with the agreement of a parent. If you believe a child has used them without it, write to us and we will delete the related data.

Display names

Display names are checked against a list of blocked words. To report an offensive name, write to us with the name and the time you saw it.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change, for example if cloud save or Google sign-in is added. The date at the top shows the latest version.
